I also mentioned the famous 'glasses' scene from the Science Fiction Film 'They Live!' where a special pair of glasses enable one to
see the truth behind mass culture and ideology. This scene has been used to describe the Culture Industry (but from today's lecture,
of course, Adorno would question whether our perceptions / how we see 'facts' can be ever separated from our embedding in
ideology) :-

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aiMLJAZajxg

This concept is explained wonderfully by social theorist Slavoj Žizek who explains, in a very 'Culture Industry' sense that we can't just
change our perspective (take on, or off our 'ideological' glasses) to see ideology as we live, and perceive it and we actively resist
attempts to do so :-

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVwKjGbz60k

Popular Culture (films, TV, music) can be a great way to think about theory in my view!
