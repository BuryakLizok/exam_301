### Style rules (apply to every answer in section 8)

- 650–750 words each
- **Each answer is preceded by its question re-stated as a sub-heading** (so the question reads with its answer when scrolled to)
- **Past-year questions tagged with their YEAR** in both the section-6 list AND the section-8 sub-heading (e.g. *"### 2023 (Q1): To what extent…"*)
- **Short direct quotes ARE permitted** when they support the argument, with full citation `(Author, *Work*, p. N)`. Page numbers I'm uncertain about marked `[VERIFY p.?]` for joint end-of-project review.

- Brief attribution on first concept use (e.g. "Foucault's notion of biopower…")
- Source weighting: presentations + readings + teacher summaries all primary (co-equal); revisions + additions + past exams secondary. Pay extra attention to slide-questions inside presentations.
- **Always include concrete examples in essay answers** — empirical cases, historical examples, contemporary illustrations, case studies. Mandatory when the prompt says "discuss with examples"; strongly recommended even when it doesn't, because past-paper style consistently rewards example-grounded arguments. Examples should map to the thinker/argument being discussed (e.g., panopticon → modern surveillance; culture industry → streaming algorithms; Black Atlantic → music & diaspora; standpoint epistemology → care work or domestic labor; etc.).
