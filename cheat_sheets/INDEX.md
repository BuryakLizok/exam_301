## ✅ Exam-day usage protocol

1. **Read all 17 questions on the paper first** (3 minutes).
2. **Pick one from each section + one wildcard.** Avoid topics where you only have thin coverage.
3. For each chosen question, **flip to the relevant file** (use this index). On the file:
   - §5 lists the past-Qs and §7 the mocks — find the closest match
   - §8 has the model essay — read once to refresh
   - Use the essay's structure (thesis → 2-4 moves → counter → conclusion) as a scaffold
   - Pull 1-2 short quotes from §4 (the file's curated quote bank)
   - **Do not copy the essay verbatim** — adapt to the exact question
4. Plan your essay in 5 minutes before writing.
5. Aim for ~750 words handwritten per essay (≈ 3 sides of A4 medium handwriting).
6. Use named concepts unquoted (they are concept-names, not quotes).
7. **One verbatim quote per essay max** (with author + work). Use sparingly.
8. Concrete examples are mandatory — they distinguish a 1st-class answer from a 2:1.
