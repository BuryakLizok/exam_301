# SC301 Reference List

**Compiled:** 2026-05-15
**Module:** SC301 Rethinking Modernity (full-year, Autumn + Spring)
**Scope:** Every author cited across the 17 lecture cheat-sheets (L1-L17).

This file lists the full bibliographic entry for every author cited in the cheat-sheets. Citations in the cheat-sheets follow Harvard format `(Author, Year: Page)`. Where multiple works by the same author appear, they are listed in chronological order.

## Notes

- Years marked with letter suffixes (e.g., `1981a`, `1990a`) distinguish multiple works by the same author in the same year.
- Editions: where the cheat-sheets cite a reprint (e.g., MacDonald 1957, Hoggart 1958), the year reflects the edition the lecture references.
- Some readers' chapter pagination differs from the original publication; pagination follows the lecture-cited edition.
- A handful of primary texts not held in `materials/` are listed here with standard bibliographic info.
